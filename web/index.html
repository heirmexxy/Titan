    Your devices see the problem. Nobody else does.

    Most operations already have sensors. The trouble is what happens to the data afterwards.

    Locked in separate dashboards

    Every device comes with its own app. Nothing joins up, and none of it talks to the systems you actually run your business on.

    You find out after the loss

    Damp grain, a failed aerator, a door left open overnight. By the time someone checks, the stock is spoiled or gone.

    Nobody can rely on the numbers

    Lenders and insurers can't base decisions on readings anyone could have edited. So good operators get treated like risky ones.

    Decisions about food and money shouldn't rest on guesswork.

    We built Titan because we needed it.

    Kitovu has run agricultural storage and supply in Nigeria since 2016. We know what it costs when produce spoils in a warehouse and no one saw it coming.

    So we built our own monitoring hardware, and a finance platform that depends on knowing the condition of stored goods. Titan is the system underneath both. Now it's open to your devices too.

    WareGuard

    Our warehouse monitor tracks temperature, humidity, produce moisture and intruders, stack by stack, and reports to Titan over Wi-Fi or GSM.

    StorageX

    Our warehouse receipt platform reads Titan's verified storage conditions, so lenders can see the state of the stock behind every receipt.

    Up and running in three steps

    1. Connect your devices

      Point any MQTT or HTTPS device at Titan, or install WareGuard. Each device gets its own key, so every reading it sends is signed.

    2. See, get alerted and act

      Watch every site on one dashboard. Get an SMS or email the moment a reading crosses a limit, and let Titan switch on the fan, pump or aerator that fixes it. Every command is logged.

    3. Share with the people who need it

      Send data to your own systems through the API. Give lenders, insurers or buyers access to exactly the sites and readings you choose, and take it back any time.

    Readings you can stand behind

    Each device holds a private key that never leaves it. Every message it sends is signed, and Titan checks the signature before accepting a single number.

    • A reading changed after it left the device fails verification and raises an alert.
    • Readings stored during a network outage keep their original time and signature.
    • Anyone you share with can check any reading against the device's public key themselves.
    • Every access to shared data is logged against the permission that allowed it.
    • Carbon results link each tonne back to the signed readings and lab certificates it came from, so verifiers can check them line by line.
